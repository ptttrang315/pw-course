descendant

1. XPath: //div[@id='parent1']/descendant::div

HTML:

<div id="parent1">
    <div id="child1">Nội dung 1</div>
    <div id="child2">Nội dung 2</div>
</div>

Result:
<div id="child1">Nội dung 1</div>
<div id="child2">Nội dung 2</div>

==============================================
2. XPath: //div[@id='grandparent2']/descendant::span

HTML:

<div id="grandparent2">
    <div id="parent2">
        <span id="child2-1">Nội dung 2-1</span>
        <span id="child2-2">Nội dung 2-2</span>
    </div>
</div>

Result:
<span id="child2-1">Nội dung 2-1</span>
<span id="child2-2">Nội dung 2-2</span>
